Exiled Casual: a free browser ARPG

Exiled Casual is a free action role-playing game that runs entirely in your web browser, with nothing to install. It draws on Path of Exile 1 and 2 both: an Atlas opened with Waystones, procedurally generated maps, click-to-move combat with skills and ailments, and deep itemization with prefix and suffix affixes, magic, rare and unique items, crafting orbs and a stash.

Characters grow through a 239-node passive tree across eight disciplines, wear visible 3D gear, and save locally in the browser. The game is an early development build and changes often. It is an original fan project, not affiliated with Grinding Gear Games. Development happens in the open at github.com/IT-BAER/exiled-casual.
