Exiled Casual – a free browser ARPG

Exiled Casual is a free action role-playing game that runs entirely in your web browser, with nothing to install. It draws on Path of Exile 1 and 2 both: Atlas map progression, procedurally generated areas, click-to-move combat, and deep itemization with prefix and suffix affixes, magic, rare and unique items.

The game is in active development and this page is its teaser. Release date: Soon™. Development happens in the open at github.com/IT-BAER/exiled-casual.
